## Mission (brochure p.1)

- Drawing on the culinary principle of *mise en place*, "everything in its place," the organization strives to inspire, train and economically elevate the next generation of culinary professionals, empowering them to positively impact their communities in and out of the kitchen.
- Through the program, Rome in a Day Productions envisions a community where real-world job training is accessible and available to all, creating career opportunities and breaking the cycle of unemployment.

## Program (/our-program/ and brochure pp.4-5)

- Serves individuals **ages 16 to 36**, preparing them for positions in the hospitality industry (/our-program/).
- Started by Chef Rome with business partner **Eileen Rhodes** after recognizing a local lack of opportunities to develop professional skills. Serves as a stepping stone for students and workforce support for area entrepreneurs and business owners (brochure p.4).
- "Nationally accredited curriculum" (brochure p.4). **CONFIRM** the accrediting body.
- Soft skills: customer service, communication styles, adaptability, teamwork and collaboration, problem solving, critical observation, conflict resolution; confident, professional, ethical conduct.
- Hard skills: knife skills, food preparation, organization, cooking, safety, table setting, financial literacy, management skills.
- Method: lectures, demonstrations, cooking and evaluations.
- Each student receives a snap jacket, a cook's cap, a knife kit and a ServSafe food handler's certificate (brochure p.4; the sentence on the brochure is garbled, **CONFIRM** wording).
- Tier One, Classroom: food terminology, sanitation licensing, the Food Pyramid, cooking basics, job readiness (appearance, attitude, work ethic).
- Tier Two, Kitchen: kitchen safety, knife skills, meal preparation, table setting.
- Tier Three, Hands-on experience: work catered events, compose menus, build and understand recipes, front and back of house best practices.
- Partners named in brochure: SAVOR... Chicago, Bon Appétit, University of Chicago.
- Session dates, length, cost, eligibility details, application steps: **not published, CONFIRM**. The concept's three "how to apply" steps are neutral placeholders.
- Outcomes / graduate numbers / placement rate: **not published, CONFIRM**. No stats are shown.
