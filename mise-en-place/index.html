Everything in its place

Training Chicago’s next generation of culinary professionals.

A three-tier hospitality training program for Chicagoans ages 16 to 36: classroom, kitchen, then hands-on work at real catered events. Led by Chef Clifford Rome in Bronzeville.

Our mission

“Investing in our future before they step up to the plate.”

Drawing on the culinary principle of mise en place, meaning “everything in its place,” the organization strives to inspire, train and economically elevate the next generation of culinary professionals, empowering them, in turn, to positively impact their communities in and out of the kitchen.

Through the program, Rome in a Day Productions envisions a community where real-world job training is accessible and available to all, creating career opportunities and breaking the cycle of unemployment.

The program

Three tiers, from the classroom to the pass.

An integrated approach that blends lectures, demonstrations, cooking and evaluations, built on a nationally accredited curriculum and updated to reflect current industry trends.

  1. 01

    Tier One: Classroom

    Introduces the basics of food terminology, sanitation licensing, the Food Pyramid, cooking basics, and job readiness, including appearance, attitude and work ethic.

  2. 02

    Tier Two: Kitchen

    Students apply classroom knowledge in a working kitchen, practicing the industry’s essential skill sets: kitchen safety, knife skills, meal preparation and table setting.

  3. 03

    Tier Three: Hands-on experience

    The final and most important stage. Trainees work catered events, compose menus, build and understand recipes, and learn best practices for both front and back of house, so they enter the workforce as capable, confident professionals.

Who it serves

Built for people ready to work.

  • Ages 16 to 36 Young people and adults who want to be exposed to, and prepared for, positions in the hospitality industry.
  • Job seekers Individuals seeking full-time employment, with basic culinary and hospitality management skills as a stepping stone.
  • Local businesses Workforce support for area entrepreneurs and business owners on Chicago’s south and west sides.

Chef Rome started the program after recognizing a local lack of opportunities to develop professional skills. Students explore different fields of the culinary trade in an intensive, practical setting.

Who teaches

Led by chefs who came up through the industry.

Founder

Chef Clifford Rome

An Englewood native who first fell for food helping his grandmother cook fresh from her garden, Chef Rome worked at Spago with Wolfgang Puck, studied culinary arts in Paris, and was one of five chefs selected to work at the Cannes International Film Festival’s American Pavilion. He founded Rome’s Joy Companies and serves on the advisory board of Washburne Culinary School.

He has partnered with the Careers Through Culinary Arts Program (CCAP), After School Matters, Healthy Schools Campaign, the YMCA, Education to Careers and Washburne Culinary School.

Co-founder

Eileen Rhodes

Chef Rome’s business partner in creating a training program that gives individuals basic culinary and hospitality management skills.

Instructor

Chef Trillis Rollins

Executive Chef of Rome’s Joy Catering and Peach’s Restaurant on 47th, with more than 15 years in the industry. As a Mise En Place instructor he mentors and trains students in a well-organized, efficient kitchen.
