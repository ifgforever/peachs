1940–1944

The ballroom opens

Opening night was March 9, 1940, with Floyd Campbell's orchestra, the house band. Club dances, school proms and battles of the bands filled the calendar, with touring stars on Sunday nights.

1960s–1970s

Saturday mornings and a Basie date

In the late 1960s, Jesse Jackson's Operation Breadbasket held its Saturday morning meetings in the ballroom, with Ben Branch's band and choir. In 1976 the Count Basie Orchestra played and recorded there.

Also on the bandstand

  • Captain Walter Dyett, DuSable High's band director, led dance orchestras at the Parkway from 1943 to 1959. He left no commercial records, so he isn't on the playlist.
  • McKie Fitzhugh, DJ and promoter, started his dances at the Parkway in 1942 and presented Duke Ellington there in 1953.
  • Fred Guy, Ellington's guitarist for over 20 years, managed the Parkway Ballroom from 1949 until his death in 1971.
  • Lonnie Simmons, jazz organist, was signed in 1958 to a Saturday radio show broadcast from the Parkway's Blue Room. Lorna Boston and LB's Blues Machine played a 2014 blues night there. Neither has a recording we could find on streaming services.
  • Mavis Staples held her first wedding reception there in 1964.

Not on the list

Lena Horne and Josephine Baker are often named as Parkway guests. The only source is a 2024 article saying they "stepped foot in" the building, and no record shows either one performing there.
