About

A chef who came home to build.

Chef Cliff Rome’s culinary experience spans the globe. While working at Spago he studied culinary arts in Paris, spent more than a year as a culinary consultant on a private yacht sailing the Caribbean, and from 1998 to 2003 was one of five chefs selected to cook at the Cannes International Film Festival’s American Pavilion, alongside Roger Vergé, Bobby Flay, Mario Batali and Todd English.

In 2000 he founded Rome’s Joy Companies. Through Mise En Place and the Clean Habits custodial program, Chef Rome has created job opportunities across Chicagoland, especially for young people of color, and he believes reinvesting in historic Bronzeville will leave a lasting, positive mark on a community with deep roots.

“Rome’s Joy Catering believes in order to maintain ecological integrity between humanity and the natural world that social equity as well as economic efficiency become a standard practice.”

    A South Side native with a BA in Performing Arts from UIC and an MBA focused on project management. She started at Rome’s Joy Catering as an administrative assistant, spent three years at McDonald’s Corporation, and came full circle as General Manager.

  • Trillis Rollins

    Executive Chef

    Executive Chef of Rome’s Joy Catering and Peach’s on 47th, with 15+ years in the industry and a BA in Graphic Design. He also mentors and trains as a Mise En Place instructor.
