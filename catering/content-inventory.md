## Request form on the live site
Fields: Name (first/last, required), Email (required), Date of Event, Number of People, Type of Event (free text), Event Location, Additional Information (“Please share any additional information about this request, including food preferences.”). A field labeled “Event Event of” is a hidden spam honeypot that is visible as a label. Intro: “Thanks for considering Rome’s Joy Catering for your event. Complete the fields below for your catering request and we’ll follow up with you soon.”

Added in the concept: Phone (optional), menu-interest checkboxes, and an event-type dropdown (Office or meeting lunch, Brunch, Dinner or celebration, Wedding or private party, Community or nonprofit event, Other). **CONFIRM** the options.
