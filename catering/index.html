How it works

From first email to the last pan.

  1. 1

    Tell us about your event

    Send the date, headcount, location and the kind of event with the quote form below, or call 773.373.4320.

  2. 2

    We follow up

    Our team follows up to talk through the menu, dietary needs and allergens, and sends your quote.

  3. 3

    Lock in the menu

    Choose your dishes and confirm the count. Per-person selections start at 10 orders each; full pans serve up to 20.

  4. 4

    We cook, you host

    Our kitchen prepares your order. Add disposables, chafing racks and sternos to set out a buffet without a kitchen; delivery and setup are confirmed with your quote.

Our kitchen

Trillis Rollins, Executive Chef

Trillis Rollins is the Executive Chef of Rome’s Joy Catering and Peach’s Restaurant on 47th. With a Bachelor of Arts in Graphic Design, he blends creativity with culinary skill for meals that are both beautiful and delicious.

With more than 15 years in the culinary industry, he designs and executes customized menus, oversees kitchen operations and keeps food preparation to top-tier standards. As a Mise en Place instructor in the hospitality training program started by Chef Rome, he also mentors and trains the next generation of cooks.

“Food is our common ground, a universal experience.”

James Beard

Founded by Chef Cliff Rome

Chef Rome founded Rome’s Joy Companies in 2000. His career runs from Spago and culinary study in Paris to five years (1998–2003) as one of five chefs at the Cannes International Film Festival’s American Pavilion. Today the companies, all in Bronzeville, include Peach’s, Rome’s Joy Catering, Parkway Social, Blanc Art Gallery, R.J.C. Sausage and the Mise en Place training program.
