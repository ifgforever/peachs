House favorites

What the regulars order.

  • Berry Pancakes

    A tall, fluffy stack under warm blueberry compote and a cloud of cream.

  • Shrimp & Grits

    Creamy grits loaded with shrimp, peppers and green onion. A Southside classic.

  • Banana French Toast

    Thick-cut and golden, piled with banana, cream and powdered sugar.

  • Hangover Chicken Wings

    Sticky, saucy wings stacked on toast. The cure for Saturday night.

  • Avocado Toast

    Smashed avocado, bright tomato, red onion, basil and a soft egg.

  • The Burger

    Toasted bun, all the fixings and a sunny egg on top. Brunch rules.
