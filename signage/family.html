One chef. One neighborhood. A whole community.

Since 2000, Chef Cliff Rome has been building restaurants, jobs, art and gathering places in Bronzeville. Together, they are Rome’s Joy Companies.

The chef behind it all

Chef Cliff Rome

An Englewood native who learned to cook beside his grandmother on the South Side.

  1. Early onCooked at Spago and studied culinary arts in Paris
  2. 1998–2003One of five chefs at the Cannes Film Festival’s American Pavilion
  3. 2000Founded Rome’s Joy Companies
  4. TodayLeads food service at the Obama Presidential Center
30+years in food service, and on the advisory board of Washburne Culinary School

The historic Parkway Ballroom, reimagined.

Once the stage for Duke Ellington, Louis Jordan and Sun Ra. Now a coworking lounge, social club and event space.

  • Membership $150 / month

In his words

“In 1939, a Black entrepreneur opened the Parkway Ballroom to celebrate Black creativity, enterprise and community.

The Parkway Ballroom was a source of elegance and dignity amid segregation and hate.

… we offer GuestBook to evoke a nostalgic reverence for what was and to honor the brilliance of today’s black entrepreneurs and artists.”

Chef Cliff RomeGuestBook, an exhibition at Blanc Gallery, 2024

Mise En Place Chicago

Training the next generation.

Started by Chef Rome and Eileen Rhodes, Mise En Place prepares Chicagoans ages 16 to 36 for careers in hospitality.

  1. 1 · ClassroomFood safety, terminology, job readiness
  2. 2 · KitchenKnife skills, prep, kitchen safety
  3. 3 · Real eventsWorking catered events, building menus

Every student gets a chef jacket, a knife kit and a ServSafe food handler certificate.

Support a student: miseenplacechicago.org · Tax-deductible

More than a business

Building community,
one person at a time.

Through job training and the arts, Chef Rome has created opportunities across Chicagoland, especially for young people of color.

1Mise En PlaceCulinary and hospitality job training for ages 16 to 36, run as a 501(c)(3) nonprofit program.
2Clean HabitsCustodial training that opens career paths beyond the kitchen.
3Rome’s Joy InstituteEducation and workforce development, recognized by the Illinois Board of Higher Education.
4Do Well / Do GoodEach exhibition cycle at Blanc Gallery lifts up two local nonprofits.
