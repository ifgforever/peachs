## Membership and pricing (/membership/)

- Membership: **$150 monthly**. Includes all spaces listed above, hi-speed internet, complimentary tea/coffee/snacks daily, parking, Kisi and Coworks apps, invitations to curated social events and mixers, 12 bookable meeting hours per month.
- Exclusive perks: 15% off meals over $30 at Peach's on 47th; 10% off MLK x Peach's merchandise and retail coffee; $1,000 off event rentals ($3,000 minimum) at Parkway Social; 20% off catered orders over $500.
- Day pass: **$25**, full day of coworking, bottomless coffee and tea.
