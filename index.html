Idea · market with other businesses

A Bronzeville screen network

Put the same kind of TV in barbershops, salons, laundromats, auto shops and waiting rooms around 47th and King, free to the business. Each screen runs that shop’s own specials and Instagram, plus a few ads from neighbors.

  • Rome’s Joy gets seen across the neighborhood: Peach’s, the catering company and Parkway Social run as local ads on every screen.
  • Partners get airtime at Peach’s: neighborhood shops can take a slot in the loops on Peach’s three TVs.
  • Community notices ride along: Blanc openings and Mise En Place enrollment, alongside weather and news.
  • Hosts can share in the ad revenue, as the flyer offers.
