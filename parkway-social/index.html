Our story

If you haven’t heard of Parkway Social… you’ve definitely heard of Parkway Ballroom.

Nestled in the heart of Bronzeville, this iconic space has long been a cultural anchor, once the stage for jazz legends like Duke Ellington, Count Basie, and Louis Armstrong. In an era of segregation, Parkway Ballroom stood as a place where Black excellence gathered, celebrated, and thrived.

Parkway Social is a member coworking lounge and social club built for connection, creativity, and cultural exchange. It’s where you can take a meeting in the morning, sip cocktails in the courtyard by sunset, and catch a live artist talk or game night after hours.

The space

A room for every kind of workday.

Settle in at a group table when you want the energy of collaboration, or choose a quiet corner when it’s time to focus. Beverage and snack stations throughout the building are stocked daily.

    A calm, elevated atmosphere with community tables and individual workstations. Complimentary pastries, snacks, premium teas, and coffee daily.

  • Writer’s Room

    Seats 8 · Reserve in the Coworks app

    A sophisticated conference suite with a lounge sofa, private restroom, and stocked mini fridge.

  • Sound Lounge

    Private · Reserve in the Coworks app

    A cozy retreat with a workstation, sofa, warm lighting, and a vintage record player for moments of focus or reset.

  • The Training Room

    Up to 20 guests · In person or hybrid

    Designed for impact: team workshops, leadership training, or hybrid presentations. Fully equipped for in-person sessions or virtual collaboration.
