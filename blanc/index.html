About the exhibition

For nearly a decade, Raja’Nee and Jalen Hamilton have built a shared practice around gathering, preserving, and transforming family memory.

Building from archival research and personal lineage, the exhibition examines the afterlives of the Great Migration and the ways Black families gather, honor, and assess what remains. Raja’Nee approaches the archive in the spirit of sankofa, reclaiming the past as a guide for the future, while Jalen translates the archive through printmaking and textiles, working from rediscovered materials as inheritance and evidence.

The exhibition features quilts and textile works that physically stitch together photographs, fabric, and archival materials: an embodied practice that transforms fragmented histories into living narratives and centers intimacy, memory, and relational knowledge.

Jalen Hamilton’s first solo exhibition, To Whom This Ground Belongs, opened at Blanc in 2022. He co-facilitated Blanc’s Deeply Curious Residency (III).

About Blanc

A Culture of Conversation

In its heyday, Bronzeville was home to the cream of Chicago’s African American society. Artists, intellectuals, and entrepreneurs engaged in a robust dialogue on matters of importance to the community.

Recent years have seen a powerful resurgence of that creative energy. Cliff Rome, proprietor of Bronzeville’s Parkway Ballroom, is committed to rekindling this Culture of Conversation: thousands have gathered at the Parkway for art exhibits, fashion events, literary readings, dance competitions, and film premieres.

With Eileen Rhodes, he founded Blanc. Its bold mission: to engage African Americans and all Chicagoans through the arts, and to ignite a dialogue on issues of spiritual, political and social significance. Blanc regularly showcases local and non-local artists in an exploration of a compelling theme.

Blanc: a center for ideas and a meeting place for people… a Culture of Conversation.

The gallery

Programs

Every exhibition is the start of a conversation. Blanc carries it into the room through two ongoing programs.

Speak Easy

Diverse events for the public: openings, artist talks, panel discussions, readings and performances.

Recent examples: a conversation with Sabrina Nelson on the legacy of James Baldwin; a fireside chat between Lawrence Agyei and the South Shore Drill Team; a Black writers’ reading of Giovanni’s Room.

Do Well / Do Good

Each cycle, Blanc identifies two local non-profits whose work intersects with the concerns of the artists, and hosts events that highlight their missions and help them raise awareness and funding.

Non-profit partners: call 773.373.4320 to start a conversation.

Private events

Blanc is not currently available for private rental. For weddings, receptions, meetings and private celebrations, look to our sister venues in Rome’s Joy Companies.
